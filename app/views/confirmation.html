What happens next

  1. Within 48 hours: Your employer will be contacted to verify your employment contract. You'll receive an email once this is confirmed.
  2. Within 7 days: Your Lunar Health Assessment certificate will be verified. If any issues are found, we'll contact you.
  3. 21 days before departure: You'll receive your pre-flight training schedule and spaceport arrival instructions.
  4. 7 days before departure: Your digital boarding pass and final travel documents will be sent to your email address.
  5. 48 hours before departure: You must check in online using your booking reference. You'll receive a reminder via text message.

Need to make changes?

If you need to change your travel date or cancel your booking:

  • Changes can be made up to 14 days before departure at no extra charge
  • Changes within 14 days of departure may incur a £100 amendment fee
  • Cancellations more than 14 days before departure will receive a full refund
  • Cancellations within 14 days of departure will receive a 50% refund

To make changes, call the Lunar Travel Helpline on 0300 123 4567 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm) or manage your booking online.
