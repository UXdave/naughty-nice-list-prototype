Book travel to the Moon for work

Use this service to book a ticket on the UK Lunar Transport Programme to travel to the Moon for employment purposes.

The UK Lunar Transport Programme provides safe, regulated travel for UK citizens who have secured employment contracts with approved lunar employers.

Who can use this service

You can apply for lunar work travel if you:

  • are a UK citizen
  • are aged 18 or over
  • have a valid employment contract with an approved lunar employer
  • hold a valid UK passport with at least 2 years remaining
  • have passed the mandatory Lunar Health Assessment

What you'll need

To complete this application, you'll need:

  • your National Insurance number
  • your UK passport number
  • your lunar employment contract reference number
  • your employer's Lunar Operations Licence number
  • your Lunar Health Assessment certificate number

Cost

The booking fee is £250. Your employer may cover this cost - check your employment contract.

The booking process takes around 15 minutes.
