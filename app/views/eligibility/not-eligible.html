You cannot use this service

{% if data['uk-citizen'] == 'no' %}

You must be a UK citizen to use the UK Lunar Transport Programme.

If you are a citizen of another country, you may be able to apply through your country's lunar travel programme, if one exists.

What you can do

  • Check if you're eligible for UK citizenship
  • Find international lunar travel programmes
{% elseif data['over-18'] == 'no' %}

You must be 18 or over to travel to the Moon for work.

This is a legal requirement under the Space Employment Act 2024.

What you can do

  • Read about lunar careers for young people
  • Find lunar education and training programmes
{% elseif data['has-employment-contract'] == 'no' %}

You must have a valid employment contract with an approved lunar employer before you can book travel.

The UK Lunar Transport Programme is for employment travel only. Tourist travel to the Moon is not currently available.

What you can do

  • Search for lunar job opportunities
  • View list of approved lunar employers
  • Get career advice for lunar employment
{% elseif data['has-health-assessment'] == 'no' %}

You must pass the Lunar Health Assessment before you can book travel to the Moon.

The assessment ensures you are physically and mentally fit for lunar travel and work conditions.

What you can do

  • Book a Lunar Health Assessment
  • Find an approved assessment centre
  • Read about what the assessment involves
